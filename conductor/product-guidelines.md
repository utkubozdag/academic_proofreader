# Product Guidelines

## Feedback Style and Tone
The feedback generated by the tool, both in Microsoft Word comments and the summary report, will adhere to a **Direct but Informative** tone.
- **Efficiency:** The primary focus is on the error and its direct fix to minimize reading time for the researcher.
- **Meaningful Explanations:** While remaining minimal, the feedback will include a brief rationale for the change. This provides just enough context for the researcher to understand the reasoning and apply the improvement independently in the future.

## Summary Report Structure
The summary report will be **Categorized by Error Type** to ensure maximum clarity and actionable insights. Findings will be grouped into logical sections such as:
- Grammar & Mechanics
- Spelling & Punctuation
- Style & Tone (Academic Voice)
- Citations & References

## Language and Terminology
Since the target audience consists of academic researchers and professors, the tool will use **Academic/Professional** terminology.
- **Precision:** Explanations will employ standard linguistic and stylistic terms (e.g., "nominalization," "antecedent agreement," "hedging") to communicate issues precisely and professionally.
- **Assumed Knowledge:** The tool assumes a high level of literacy and familiarity with formal writing conventions.
